# Privacy Policy

This static fixture describes a fictional notes application used to evaluate reinhardt. It is not a real policy.

We store your account, notes and profile in Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Supabase processes the content you save, your email address and technical request data so that we can provide the service.

When you use the assistant feature, the text of your request and relevant note content are sent to OpenAI to generate a reply. We do not use OpenAI to build advertising profiles.

We keep account data while your account is open and delete it within thirty days after you close your account. You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time by contacting the fictional support address for this fixture.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use advertising or session recording tools in this application.
