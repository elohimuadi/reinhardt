# Privacy policy

This demonstration application explains the personal information it processes and the choices offered to people who use it. The statements in this fixture describe a fictional application and exist to test static detection.

We receive account details you submit while signing in, support messages you choose to send, and technical request information needed to operate the application. We use that information to provide requested features, respond to questions, and investigate reliability problems. Access to account records is limited to people who need it to provide those services.

We retain information for the time needed to provide the requested service, resolve support issues, and honor deletion requests. You can contact the application operator to ask for access, correction, or deletion of your account details. Some records may remain in backups until their normal expiry. Changes to this policy will be described on this page before materially different processing begins.

With your permission, we use PostHog to measure page usage. We disable session recording and automatic event capture in this example. Stripe processes purchases and receives transaction and billing information when you make a payment.
